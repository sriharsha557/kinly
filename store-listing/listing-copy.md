## Full description (4000 chars max)

```
Tracking was never the problem.

Everyone already knows what they should do — exercise, read, save, learn. Habit apps hand you a checkbox for it, and most people still abandon them within weeks. A checkbox never touches the actual problem: staying consistent once motivation runs out.

Kinly assumes the reason you stop isn't a missing feature. It's that nobody's watching.

🌱 ONE LOOP, EVERY DAY

• Start a circle — invite 2–10 friends you actually trust into a private Growth Circle.
• Set your goals — health, wealth, ideas, learning, or relationships.
• Log progress — check in and answer the circle's daily prompt. A minute or two, most days.
• Watch it grow — streaks, gardens, and circle activity, all in one place.
• Show up for them — nudge a streak, check in on someone who's gone quiet, start a challenge together.

🪴 EVERY GOAL PLANTS SOMETHING

Kinly turns consistency into something you can actually see. Log a goal and your garden moves forward. Go quiet, and it wilts — no lectures, just a plant that notices.

👥 BUILT LIKE A TRUSTED ROOM

Kinly isn't a social network with a smaller radius. It's closer to a group chat: what happens in a circle stays in that circle, and nothing about it is designed to be public.

• No followers, no likes from strangers, no public profiles
• Invite-only circles of 2–10 people
• No ads, no tracking SDKs, no data resale

WHAT'S INSIDE

• Today — your one clear answer to "what should I do right now": your circle's health at a glance, today's goals, and what your circle's been up to.
• Circle — every member's garden, side by side. Pair up with an accountability buddy and take on challenges together.
• Goals — set a target, log progress, keep the streak alive. Step goals can track automatically through Health Connect, so a walk counts without you logging anything.
• Together — one daily prompt for the whole circle, answers hidden until you post your own. Plus two-minute games for lighter days.
• Profile — your streaks, your badges, your story. Mood check-ins that take ten seconds. Future Letters your circle can never read.
• Nudges — one-tap cheers when a friend's on a streak or gone quiet. A small push, never a lecture.

WHY IT WORKS

Most habit trackers are built for an audience of one. You track alone, so nobody sees when you quit. In Kinly, your friends see your consistency — and your quiet weeks too. That gentle visibility is the whole point: not pressure, just people who notice.

Grab a few friends. Pick your goals. Grow together.
```
