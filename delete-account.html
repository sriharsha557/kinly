Account

Delete your Kinly account

You can delete your Kinly account and its data at any time, either directly in the app or by emailing us. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Kinly and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Settings, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — By email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address your account is registered under, with the subject "Delete my account":

kinlyappsupport@gmail.com

We'll verify the request and complete the deletion within 30 days (usually much sooner), and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile (name, email, bio, avatar)
  • Your goals, check-ins, streaks, mood check-ins, and step-goal data
  • Your posts, replies, nudges, Vision Board images, and Future Letters
  • Your circle memberships: you're removed from every circle
  • Your push-notification token

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Underlying records may persist in encrypted backups for a short period before being permanently erased; crash reports already sent to our error-reporting service expire automatically per its retention window.

What may remain

Like leaving a group chat, content that other circle members replied to may remain visible to them in anonymized form (shown without your name or profile). Nothing that remains is linked to your identity.

Questions

Anything unclear, or a partial-deletion request (e.g. delete step data but keep the account)? Email us, and see our Privacy Policy for the full picture of what we store.
